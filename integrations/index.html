Google-connected internal tools

Empyrean Integrations

Connected tools for authorized users to work with their documents, communications, research, and advertising reports.

Empyrean Integrations is the public information page for a collection of internal Google-connected tools administered by Jacob Jones. The shared Google application has also used the name gdrive-mcp. This page is hosted by Empyrean Consulting; it is not a public sign-up service.

The Google Ads reporting integration serves Empyrean Ventures Inc., doing business as Ellie Mental Health, for its Hales Corners and Waukesha StoneRidge clinics. Empyrean Consulting and the clinic business are identified separately. This is not an application operated by Ellie corporate or by Google.

Read the integrations privacy policy for the data each feature uses, how it is handled, and how to disconnect or request deletion.

What the integrations do

Google Drive and documents

Find and read authorized files and folders; create folders, upload files, and update documents when requested. The local Drive connector applies a configured folder allowlist. Connected assistants can use selected content to answer questions and help prepare work products.

Gmail and research workflows

Help authorized users find relevant correspondence, organize research material, and prepare messages. Separate connectors have different permissions: a compose-only sender does not gain inbox-reading access. Outbound messages default to drafts; sending requires explicit human approval.

Google Chat connectors

Where enabled and authorized, connected tools use spaces, membership information, message content, and read state for conversation context, and can compose messages under the applicable approval controls. These permissions belong to the Chat feature, not to the Ads integration.

Google Ads reporting for the clinics

The current EIP integration reads account and campaign information and advertising performance to support internal reporting and assessment for the clinic account. Campaign mutation through EIP is not implemented. It does not currently change ads, budgets, bids, or account access.

Google Ads API access levels do not override application controls or grant access to another user's Google services. Patient lists and protected health information must not be submitted to this integration.

Separate permissions, separate purposes

Only the feature and Google account you authorize determine what a connector can access. Sharing a Google Cloud project does not combine your accounts or give every tool every permission. Model-provider authentication, including Gemini, and sign-in to business applications are separate from these data-access permissions.

These tools are for authorized adults conducting internal work. They are not patient portals and do not offer clinical services. Clinic information is available on the official Hales Corners and Waukesha StoneRidge pages.
