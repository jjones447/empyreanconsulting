EVI EIP Books

An internal QuickBooks Online connection for accounting reports and reconciliation at Empyrean Ventures, Inc. (EVI).

Empyrean LLC is the application publisher and integration operator. Empyrean Ventures, Inc. is the separate business whose QuickBooks company may be connected. This is not a public sign-up service, an Ellie corporate application, or a patient portal.

What the connection is for

The first intended EIP use is to read the selected QuickBooks company's identity, chart of accounts, general ledger and trial balance for internal reporting and tie-out. The pilot does not offer payment processing, payroll, bank-feed changes, or journal posting. A broader function would require a separate review of permissions, controls and these public disclosures.

Only an authorized QuickBooks administrator can approve company access through Intuit's consent flow. The current setup is not yet a live production connection. This information page does not initiate OAuth or receive authorization codes.

Policies and support

Read the QuickBooks integration privacy notice and use terms. The separate Google integrations privacy policy does not govern QuickBooks data.
