X-Centric XIP Books

An internal QuickBooks Online connection for accounting, invoicing and project reporting at X-Centric IT Solutions, inside XIP, X-Centric's internal work platform.

Empyrean LLC is the application publisher and integration operator. X-Centric IT Solutions is the separate business whose QuickBooks company may be connected. This is not a public sign-up service.

What the connection is for

XIP reads the selected QuickBooks company's identity, preferences, chart of accounts, customers, products and services, invoices, and the profit and loss, balance sheet, general ledger and trial balance reports, for internal reporting, project financials and reconciliation.

XIP can also create customers, invoices or journal entries, but only when an authorized X-Centric person approves each one; every created record is read back from QuickBooks to confirm it. The connection does not process payments, run payroll, change bank feeds or file taxes.

Only an authorized QuickBooks administrator can approve company access through Intuit's consent flow. This information page does not initiate OAuth or receive authorization codes.

Policies and support

Read the privacy notice and use terms for this connection. The separate Google integrations privacy policy and the EVI EIP Books pages do not govern X-Centric QuickBooks data.
